How crime compares

Two precincts that overlap the district (the 18th and 14th) draw so much daytime traffic that crime rates per 100,000 residents cannot readily be compared with the borough or city. Of the district’s four other precincts, one has less crime per resident than Manhattan, and none less than the city as a whole.
