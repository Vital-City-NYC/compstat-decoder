Year-to-date, crime is up in 3 of the 4 precincts in your council district.

How crime is trending

NYPD released data on criminal complaints through August 16, 2026. The department reports crime by police precinct, and in the four precincts that overlap Council District 6, crime is up in three. The biggest single factor is felony assault, up 26.8% on average across the district’s precincts.

How precincts in your district compare

Two precincts that overlap the district (the 22nd and 18th) draw so much daytime traffic that crime rates per 100,000 residents cannot readily be compared with the borough or city. Of the district’s two other precincts, all have less crime per resident than Manhattan and the city as a whole. Citywide, the rate of total major crimes is 805 per 100,000 residents, year-to-date.
